Paeds Hip & Spine Course

Under construction. This course is still being written and has not yet been reviewed by orthopaedic staff or residents. Each lesson shows its sources and its review status.

A free introduction to paediatric hip and spine conditions, for anyone interested in learning more about the subject. Work through it in order: each lesson teaches one idea at a time, with short quizzes along the way. Thresholds vary between centres.

About this site

This course is made by a medical graduate who started the project as a way to teach themselves orthopaedics. New topics are added as they are learned. It is written with the help of Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Facts are checked against Miller's Review of Orthopaedics (8th ed., 2019) and Netter's Concise Orthopaedic Anatomy (2nd ed., 2009), with page references; anything not from Miller's is marked "(not in Miller's)". The text and diagrams are original. The aim is to have every lesson reviewed by orthopaedic surgeons; each lesson shows its review status at the top and will be updated as reviews are done.

Disclaimer: this site is for education only. It is not medical advice and must not be used to diagnose or treat any patient. If you are worried about a child, see a doctor.
