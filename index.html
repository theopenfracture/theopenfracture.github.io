About and review

Education should be free. The Open Fracture is open access: no paywall, no sign-up, nothing to buy.

It is made by a medical graduate who started the project as a way to teach themselves orthopaedics, and new topics are added as they are learned. It is written with the help of Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Facts are checked against Miller's Review of Orthopaedics (8th ed., 2019) and Netter's Concise Orthopaedic Anatomy (2nd ed., 2009), with page references; anything not from Miller's is marked "(not in Miller's)". The text and diagrams are original.

The aim is to have every lesson reviewed by orthopaedic surgeons. Each lesson shows its review status at the top, like the authors of a paper, and says Pending until it has been checked.
